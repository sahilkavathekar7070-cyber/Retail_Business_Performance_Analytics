## 🎯 Project Overview

The **Retail Business Performance Analytics** project focuses on analyzing retail transaction data to understand sales performance, profitability, product contribution, and category-level trends.

Using Microsoft Excel, Power Query, Power Pivot, PivotTables, and interactive dashboard elements, I transformed raw transaction data into meaningful business insights that can support better decision-making.

The project covers the complete data analysis workflow, from data preparation and transformation to exploratory analysis, visualization, and business recommendations.

## ❓ Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large volumes of transaction data, but raw data alone does not provide a clear picture of business performance.

Key questions include:

- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which product categories perform well or poorly?
- How are sales, profit, and quantity changing?
- Which products contribute most to overall revenue?
- How can these insights support inventory and business planning?

This project uses Excel-based analysis and visualization to answer these questions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Clean and transform 50,000+ retail transaction records.
- Analyze sales, profit, and quantity across 7 product categories.
- Identify top-performing products and business segments.
- Build an interactive dashboard to monitor key performance indicators.
- Generate actionable insights for inventory planning and business decisions.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1. **Top Product Contribution:** The dashboard analysis identified the Top 5 products as contributing **61.18% of total revenue**, highlighting the importance of key revenue-generating products.
2. **Category Performance:** Sales and profit analysis across 7 categories helps identify strong and weak business segments.
3. **Product Performance:** Product-level analysis highlights items that contribute significantly to revenue.
4. **Inventory Planning:** Sales and quantity trends can help businesses review stock allocation and prioritize products.

<details>
<summary><b>Data validation note</b></summary>

A direct aggregation of `Total_Amount` in the uploaded 50,000-row CSV gives a Top 5 product revenue contribution of approximately **57.94%**, rather than 61.18%.

The 61.18% figure is retained above as the result supplied for the dashboard. Before publishing, verify that both calculations use the same revenue measure, filters, and product-ranking logic.

</details>

## 📌 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions can be considered:

- Prioritize inventory review for products that consistently generate high revenue.
- Investigate low-performing categories to understand possible improvement areas.
- Monitor profit alongside revenue to avoid focusing only on high-sales products.
- Use quantity trends to support stock allocation and replenishment decisions.
- Review dashboard filters regularly to compare different product and category segments.
